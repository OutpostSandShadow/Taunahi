## 🌟 Overview

**Taunahi** is an all-in-one automation and utility client built specifically for the **Hypixel SkyBlock Garden**. With realistic farming scripts, a full Discord-connected dashboard, and a suite of quality-of-life tools, Taunahi handles everything from crop automation to profit tracking, letting you farm smarter with minimal manual input.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

### 🌾 Farming Scripts
* **Wart & Crops**: Realistic scripts for automating hours of farming with no downtime, supporting a wide range of farm designs.
* **Pumpkin & Melon**: A great option for both XP and money.
* **Sugarcane / Flowers**: Our Sugarcane script now also supports the new Garden flower crops, with high profits.
* **Cactus**: One of the best options for money, with consistently high prices.
* **Cocoa Bean**: A solid, reliable choice for money.
* **Mushroom**: The best crop to farm for XP, by far.

### ⚙️ Automation Modules
* **Echo**: Mimics farming for any farm design, letting you use custom layouts outside of our presets.
* **Pests**: Seamlessly clears pests from the garden, with automatic spraying and repellent use.
* **Visitors**: Boosts profit/hour by completing visitor requests in-between reward cycles.
* **Farm Builders**: Speeds up your Garden workflow by automatically constructing your required farms.

### 🖥️ Dashboard & Discord Integration
* **Scripting Sessions**: Tracks statistics and events for every session, viewable in your dashboard — total earnings, failsafes, screenshots, and more.
* **Discord Integration**: Sends notifications, screenshots, and hourly rates straight to your private Discord channel.
* **Remote Control**: Control your script directly from your dashboard or Discord, no PC access required.

### 📊 Tracking & Selling
* **Statistics & Profit Calculator**: Tracks earnings, skill XP, scripting time, and more, so you always know your numbers.
* **Auto-Sell**: Automatically sells collected items to whichever is more profitable — NPC or Bazaar.

### 🛠️ Reliability Tools
* **Instant Anti-Stuck**: Gets you out of blocks quickly and smoothly.
* **Failsafe**: Handles hub switches, disconnects, and other interruptions automatically.
* **Mouse Control**: Includes Mouse Lock, Block Checker, Tool Switcher, and more.
* **Rewarper**: Automatically rewarps you to your spawnpoint when teleport pads aren't available.

### 🎨 Interface
* **Beautiful Menu & HUD**: A clean, fluid, and fully customizable menu and on-screen display.
* **Humanized Movement**: Movement and rotation based on your own mouse sensitivity and behavioral factors, rather than a fixed simulated pattern.

---

## ⚡ Getting Started

1. **Download & Install**: Place the client files into your mods folder.
2. **Launch**: Open your supported Minecraft client and load Taunahi.
3. **Configure**: Open the in-game menu to set up your farm designs, failsafes, and Discord integration.
4. **Enjoy**: Let Taunahi handle the grind while you track your profits from the dashboard.
